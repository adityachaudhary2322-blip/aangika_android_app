# Aangika: Sign Language Translator (Android)

Indian Sign Language to speech, speech to text, and signed video calls, with
an ASL translator for international users. Made by team HealX.

## Download

**[⬇ Download the latest Aangika APK](https://github.com/adityachaudhary2322-blip/aangika_android_app/releases/latest/download/aangika.apk)**

All versions: [Releases](https://github.com/adityachaudhary2322-blip/aangika_android_app/releases)

### Install on your phone (Android 6.0 or newer)

1. Open the link above on your phone and download `aangika.apk` (about 74 MB).
2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser or Files app to
   **install unknown apps**.
3. Google Play Protect may say the app is unrecognised, because it is not from
   the Play Store. Tap **More details → Install anyway**.
4. Open **Aangika** and allow the camera (and microphone for speech to text).

The sign recognition models are inside the app, so recognition works offline.
Sarvam sentences, translation and voices need an internet connection.

**Updating:** download the new APK and install it over the old one; your
settings and taught signs are kept.

## What it does

- **Sign to speech:** sign in front of the camera, live or record-then-play.
- **ASL Translator** (for international users): 250 everyday ASL signs.
- **Speech to text:** live captions of what people say; typed replies read aloud.
- **My signs:** teach your own signs. **Phrases**, **Messages**, **Meet** (group calls).
- Choose between recognition models (ISL tagger, ISL handshapes, ASL 250).

Accuracy is measured and stated honestly in the app (Settings → Recognition).
